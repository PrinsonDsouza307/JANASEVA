CATEGORY A TEMP DATA

Clear Temporary Data

Flushes transient offline drafts, IndexedDB queues, and transmission logs without affecting core data.

Purged:

  • IndexedDB offline submission queue & drafts
  • Session storage and browser cached complaints
  • Email transmission audit logs & WhatsApp logs

Preserved:

  • All citizen complaints and tracking IDs
  • All citizen, receiver, and admin profiles
CATEGORY B COMPLAINTS

Clear Grievance Records

Operational lifecycle cleanse. Purges grievance transactions and messages while keeping all user accounts intact.

Purged:

  • All citizen complaints and tracking IDs
  • Status progression histories and official responses
  • Email and WhatsApp delivery logs

Preserved:

  • Citizen, Receiver, and Administrator accounts
  • Panchayat directory and system settings
CATEGORY C CITIZENS

Reset Citizen Data

Purges registered citizen user accounts and citizen-filed grievances. Official Panchayat receivers remain active.

Purged:

  • Registered citizen accounts (role: citizen)
  • Grievance records submitted by citizens

Preserved:

  • Official Gram Panchayat Receiver logins
  • Administrator account
CATEGORY D RECEIVERS

Reset Receiver Data

Restores receiver profiles and email configurations to standard directory defaults.

Purged:

  • Custom receiver settings and notification preferences
  • Receiver email override configurations

Preserved:

  • Citizen accounts and citizen grievances
  • Super Administrator account
CATEGORY E FULL RESET

Full Application Data Reset

Complete factory reset baseline. Purges all operational data & citizen registrations. Super Admin is strictly preserved.

Purged:

  • All complaints, subcollections & review notes
  • All citizen accounts and delivery logs

Strictly Preserved:

  • Super Admin — protected
  • Verified cryptographic backup snapshots & audit ledger
CATEGORY F RESTORE

Restore from Backup

Disaster recovery restoration. Reinstates system state from a verified pre-reset snapshot with SHA-256 validation.

Recovery Workflow:

  • Select cryptographic pre-reset snapshot from table
  • Recomputes & validates SHA-256 hash integrity
  • Atomically restores grievances, users, and audit records

Full data integrity protection with automatic rollback on error.
