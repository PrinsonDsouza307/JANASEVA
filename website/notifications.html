Active Dispatch

Whenever you register a civic grievance on JANASEVA, our automated notification engine sends instant email updates at each official Gram Panchayat lifecycle transition:

1. Submission & Slip
Instant electronic acknowledgement with permanent Complaint ID and QR tracking slip.
2. Panchayat Review
Notification as soon as the village Executive Officer assigns your grievance to a field officer.
3. Field Remediation
Real-time progress alerts, site inspection logs, and physical repair progress notes.
4. Resolution & Report
Final sign-off with official Panchayat resolution report and citizen feedback prompt.
