About JANASEVA

A digital platform to connect citizens with their Gram Panchayat for quick and transparent grievance redressal.

Our Objective

JANASEVA aims to provide a simple, transparent, and convenient way for citizens to report local issues to their Gram Panchayat and track their resolution online.

Key Features

Submit Complaint

Report village-level issues with a photo.

Track Status

Check the real-time progress of your complaint.

Panchayat Directory

Find contact details of Gram Panchayats in your area.

Get Updates

Receive status updates on your registered email.
Empowering rural citizens through a simple and digital grievance system.

About the Portal

Portal Name : JANASEVA
Type : Gram Panchayat Grievance Portal
Purpose : Citizen grievance redressal at the village level
Users : Village citizens and Gram Panchayat officials
Coverage : All Gram Panchayats (configurable)
Version : 1.0.0 (Production Release)

Our Vision

To build cleaner, safer, and better villages through citizen participation and timely action by the Gram Panchayat.
